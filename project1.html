WildBuds Rebranding Visual Identity

A full visual identity rebrand for Wildbuds Safaris — a new logo system, brand collateral, and key art built around a bold, adventurous safari aesthetic.

Client
Wildbuds Safaris
Role
Rebrand & Brand Identity
Year
2026
Tools
Photoshop, Figma

Giving Wildbuds Safaris a brand as memorable as the trips it sells.

Wildbuds Safaris needed a visual identity that could carry the emotional weight of the experience it sells — encountering wildlife in its own habitat. The rebrand centers on an acacia-tree-and-elephant mark paired with the line “Forever in Mind,” built to work across everything from business cards to full campaign key art.

The system includes light and dark logo variations across a warm, earth-toned palette, a full stationery suite, and photography-led key art for marketing use.
