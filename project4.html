Wondr Social Media Content

Social media and campaign content for Wondr, an original streetwear concept brand — built from the ground up around bold typography and a raw, editorial visual language.

Client
Wondr
Role
Content Strategy
Year
2025
Tools
Photoshop

A concept streetwear brand, built entirely from scratch.

Wondr isn't an existing label — it's an original concept brand, created and developed around clothing and the culture of streetwear. That gave the content more room to be bold: oversized type, high-contrast layouts, and a raw, zine-like feel that matches the streetwear space it lives in.

The content spans drop announcements, sale campaigns, and lookbook-style editorial posts, all designed to feel native to a streetwear brand's social feed rather than like traditional advertising.
