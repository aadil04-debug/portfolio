Azura Brand Identity

A brand identity for Azura, a modest-wear fashion label — built to hold contemporary style and thoughtful, high-quality modest design in the same frame.

Client
Azura
Role
Brand Identity
Year
2025
Tools
Photoshop, Illustrator, Figma

A modest-wear brand built on the idea that elegance needs no compromise.

AZURA was born from a simple yet profound realization: that modesty and modern style are not mutually exclusive. We believe that true elegance lies in confidence, comfort, and self-expression, regardless of coverage.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between contemporary fashion trends and the need for thoughtful, high-quality modest wear.
